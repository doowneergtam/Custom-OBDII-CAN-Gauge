1:1 pixels at 466 px · 44.5 mm active area

Encoder: rotate moves the selection ring between cells (it fades after 3 s), click resets that cell's min and max, hold opens the menu; in the menu rotate scrolls, click selects, hold backs out. Touch: tap a cell to select it, tap it again to reset, swipe to move, hold for the menu; in the menu tap the middle row to choose, tap above or below or swipe to scroll. Keyboard: arrows, Enter, Space. Mouse wheel rotates.

Zones live on the channel, so every cell showing it colours the same way. Edit them in the channel editor later; the preset values here are the ones I'd ship.

Bar fill

Gradient: the whole bar is one colour, picked from a smooth blend through the zone colours at the tip's position, so it drifts from blue through green to yellow and red as the value climbs. Segments colours each block by its own zone. Solid snaps the whole bar to the zone the value is in.

Tone per channel and level

channelwarnbad

An alarm fires once on entering a zone, and bad zones can repeat while the value stays there. Channels on screen sound their tone; the cell shows the state. Every other channel with a tone set is watched silently and raises a three second banner naming it and its value. On the board this plays through the ES8311 codec and the onboard amp.
